what is roses and champagne?

Roses and Champagne is a completed novel series with four books in total written by ZIG.
First published in 2011 as a physical copy, it was later published by Yomi Books in Ridibooks in 2017 as a web novel.
It has been adapted into an ongoing webtoon, which is written by ZIG and illustrated by Ttung Gae.
The manhwa was released in 2021 and was originally published in KENAZ and serialized in Bomtoon and Lezhin.
